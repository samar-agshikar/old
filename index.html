THE 1

Open the-1.app ↗

Started in June 2026, THE 1 replaces scattered timetables, note apps, task lists, and generic AI chat tabs with a unified academic workspace.

  • School Pilot (10/2026): Conducted THE 1’s school pilot at Adani International School to observe real student workflows.
  • Product Hunt Launch (10/2026): Launched publicly on Product Hunt following refinements from the school pilot.
  • Unified Context: Connects timetables, subject notes, homework, and spaced revision with context-aware AI tools.

Engineering with clarity.
Built for real use.

I build complete, browser-first applications—prioritizing clean workspace state modeling, fast deterministic workflows, and grounded AI integration over superficial demos.

HTML5 · CSS · JavaScript / TypeScript · WebGL Shaders · LLM Context Workflows

Unified Workspace State

Structuring schedules, notes, deadlines, and revision sessions around a shared subject context so information stays connected.

Deterministic Core, Grounded AI

Keeping daily student organization reliable and deterministic while allowing AI features in THE 1 and Kerniq to reference explicit user notes.

Pilot-Led Iteration

Testing THE 1 directly with students at Adani International School prior to the Product Hunt launch to remove real workflow friction.
