01 · Flagship Workspace Started 06/2026 · ADIS Pilot & Product Hunt Launch 10/2026

THE 1

An all-in-one student workspace unifying class schedules, structured notes, homework, spaced revision, and context-aware AI tools.

the-1.app ↗

Problem & Motivation

Academic workflows are routinely split across separate timetable apps, note editors, task lists, and AI chat windows that have no context about what a student is currently studying.

Product Vision

Started in June 2026, THE 1 brings schedules, subject notes, revision cycles, and context-grounded AI study assistance into a single workspace at the-1.app.

Workspace Context Architecture
02 · Ecosystem Intelligence Started 11/2026 · Active Development

Kerniq

kerniq.the-1.app ↗

AI & Learning Philosophy

Started in November 2026, Kerniq explores how AI can support structured understanding rather than shortcutting thought—helping learners break concepts into clear logical stages and build lasting mental models.

Integration with THE 1

Developed at kerniq.the-1.app alongside THE 1, serving as a dedicated environment for conceptual synthesis and guided study workflows.
